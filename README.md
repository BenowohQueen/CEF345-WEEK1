Market Stock Management System

1. Project Description

The Market Stock Management System is a software application designed to help shop owners and market managers manage their products and stock efficiently. It aims to simplify inventory management by tracking product quantities, sales, purchases, and available stock.

2. Project Objectives

* To maintain accurate records of products.
* To monitor available stock quantities.
* To record product sales and purchases.
* To reduce errors in manual stock management.
* To make inventory management easier and more efficient.

3. Proposed Features

* Product registration and management.
* Stock quantity tracking.
* Recording sales transactions.
* Recording purchases and stock replenishment.
* Monitoring available products.

4. Target Users

* Shop owners.
* Market managers.
* Storekeepers.
* Inventory administrators.
